# College Pick'em Rules

## Picks

- Only admin-selected games are displayed.
- The number of games varies by week.
- Players make one pick for each selected game.
- Once a pick is made, it cannot be removed.
- A pick may be changed until that game's kickoff.
- Picks lock individually at kickoff.

## Tiebreaker

- The tiebreaker is a separate section.
- The tiebreaker game may also be one of the selected games.
- Players only enter the predicted combined total points.
- Players do not pick a winner for the tiebreaker.

## Game Display

- Show the game name when available.
- Display kickoff time in the user's local timezone.
- Away team is always on the left.
- Home team is always on the right.
- Show "Away" and "Home" above each team.
- Show ranking beside the team name.
- Show the team logo.
- Show the betting line (+/-) at the bottom of each team card.

## Home Screen

The home screen should answer:

- Do I still have picks to make?
- When do picks lock?
- How am I doing this week?
